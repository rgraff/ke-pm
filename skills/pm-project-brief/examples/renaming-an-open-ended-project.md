# Example — the KE project is really an initiative

The name fails the "can you tell when it is done?" test, and that turns out to
be a scoping problem rather than a wording one.

---

**User:** `/ke-pm:pm-project-brief` for "Improve Boat Ed onboarding"

**Claude:** *(reads the List description)*
> Before the interview — the name is worth a minute. "Improve Boat Ed
> onboarding" has no end state. You could keep improving onboarding forever, so
> nobody can say when this KE project is finished.
>
> That usually means one of two things: it is an initiative rather than a KE
> project, or there is a specific bounded piece inside it. Which is this?

**User:** hm. I guess we have three things we want to do — the welcome email,
the first-lesson flow, and the account setup form.

**Claude:**
> Then that is three KE projects, and "Improve Boat Ed onboarding" is the
> umbrella they sit under — a good initiative name, but not something a Brief
> gets written for.
>
> Which of the three are you starting?

**User:** the first-lesson flow, that's where we lose the most people

**Claude:**
> Something like:
>
> - **Rebuild Boat Ed first-lesson flow**
> - **Reduce drop-off in Boat Ed first lesson**
>
> The second names the outcome, the first names the work. Which fits?

**User:** the second

**Claude:**
> One catch: the List is currently named "Improve Boat Ed onboarding" and the
> List description matches. Renaming now is easy; after the name has been in weekly
> updates and Slack posts it is a nuisance. Rename the List to "Reduce drop-off
> in Boat Ed first lesson"?

**User:** yes do that

**Claude:** *(updates the List name and the List description, then starts the
interview for the narrower KE project)*
> Done. The other two pieces get their own Lists when they start.
>
> Now the interview. Where in the first lesson do people drop off?

---

**What this example shows:**

- The name check catches a scoping problem, not a wording problem.
- The oversized "project" is recognised as an umbrella with real KE projects
  under it, instead of being renamed into something that sounds bounded but is
  not.
- The rename happens now, with the reason stated.
- The two pieces not being started now are named and left for later, rather
  than folded into this KE project to keep them together.
